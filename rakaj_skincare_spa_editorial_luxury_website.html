About Rakaj Skincare & Spa

Unhurried Care Designed Around Your Skin Barrier

At Rakaj Skincare & Spa, we believe radiant, healthy skin cannot be rushed. Located at Suite 4, Mini Shopping Complex, opposite Lino Supermarket, Similoluwa Street, Ado-Ekiti, our studio offers an intimate alternative to crowded salons.

From deep-cleansing ozone steam facials and gentle extractions to infrared/LED light therapy, ultrasonic skin scrubbing, and curated homecare regimens, every visit is guided by experienced, customer-friendly estheticians.
